# Booking.com link: what to do (no app changes needed)

## What is happening
- In Booking.com, each "calendar connection" works both ways. Booking gives you a link to export your bookings, and you give it a link to import from another site.
- "Cleaning calendar" says "Activating" because the import half is still empty. Until it's finished, the link you copied from it doesn't work, so the app can't sync it.
- The "airbnb" row already brings your Airbnb bookings into Booking.com.

## Steps (one connection covers both jobs)
1. In Booking.com, click **Complete setup** on "Cleaning calendar".
2. Paste your **Airbnb** calendar link (the one already in the app's Settings, from airbnb.com) and name it "Airbnb". Click Done.
3. Then remove the old "airbnb" row, so Airbnb isn't imported twice.
4. Wait until "Cleaning calendar" shows **OK**. This can take a few minutes.
5. In the app, tap **Sync now** again. You don't need to change the Booking.com link in Settings.
